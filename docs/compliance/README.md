# Compliance docs — UAE PDPL

Drafts for items #7, #8, #9 of the compliance fix list. These are the **paper**
deliverables (the code fixes — consent line, in-UAE inference, retention job —
live in the app, not here).

**Legal basis:** UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL), as operationalised
by its Executive Regulations (**Cabinet Decision No. 111 of 2023**). The PDPL is
in force and enforced by the **UAE Data Office (UDO)**; all organisations
processing UAE residents' personal data must be compliant.

| File | Item | What it is |
|------|------|------------|
| [`breach-response.md`](breach-response.md) | #7 | Breach notification procedure (PDPL Art. 9) |
| [`data-roles-and-dpa.md`](data-roles-and-dpa.md) | #8 | Controller/processor split + DPA template (Art. 1, 8) |
| [`dpia.md`](dpia.md) | #9 | Data Protection Impact Assessment + DPO note (Art. 10, 21) |

> **These are drafts, not legal advice.** Article mappings are grounded in the
> PDPL and its Executive Regulations, but the wording — especially the DPA in #8
> and the final DPIA sign-off in #9 — must be reviewed by a UAE-qualified
> data-protection lawyer before any real broker relies on them. Having drafts
> means the lawyer edits instead of starting from a blank page.

## When each is needed

- **Before any real broker goes live:** #7 (breach procedure).
- **Before broker #2:** #8 (roles/DPA — sets who is liable) and #9 (DPIA + DPO,
  which trigger at multi-agent scale).
